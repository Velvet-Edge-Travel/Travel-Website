01Day by dayItinerary overview
Day 16 October 2027

Arrive in Marrekesh, golf at Al Maaden

Flight from London landing in Marrakech-RAK - local transfer in Marrakesh airport will meet the group lead to take the group to the Villas. Once checked in and refreshed there will be late breakfast/early lunch awaiting prepared by the Chef, the transfer will pick you up at 1pm for your first round of golf, to arrive at Al Maaden Golf Club. After your round this will be followed by your first evening meal prepared by the Chef. Due to timings there will be 2 main meals for the first day, and snacks for anytime of the day post dinner.

Day 27 October 2027

Golf at Assoufid

Day 2 will start with breakfast, and a early start to your 2nd round of golf, the transfer will pick you up from the Villa. You will be playing Golf at Assoufid Golf Club. Post the round refreshing beverages and food can be purchased at he club house before the transfer picks you up to return back to the villas. Late lunch will be awaiting - the afternoon free to lounge, sight see, plunge in the pool until dinner is served. A free evening at your leisure.

Day 38 October 2027

Fairmont Royal Golf and Country club

Breakfast prepared by the chef will be available, to then have a midday tee off at the infamous Fairmont Royal Golf and Country club, post the round the transfer will pick you up to take you back to the villas. Dinner will be served with snacks available later in the night.

Day 49 October 2027

Noria Golf Club

Breakfast will be available early, to then have a mid morning tee off at Noria Golf Club , post the round the transfer will pick you up to take you back to the villas. Lunch and Dinner will be served.

Day 510 October 2027

Departure

Transfer to pick you up after breakfast to take you to the airport for your flight back to London.

Our contempory luxury villas on the prestigious Al Maaden Golf Resort take their inspiration from the Moroccan architectural materials and traditional lifestyle.
