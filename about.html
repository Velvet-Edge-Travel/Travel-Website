About Velvet Edge

Curiosity is our compass.

We travel deeply, listen closely and design every journey as if it were our own.

Our beginning

Born from a love of going further

Velvet Edge began with a simple belief: the best travel experiences are personal, not packaged.

After two decades spent discovering extraordinary hotels, remote landscapes and the people who bring a place to life, our founders created a different kind of travel company. One built around conversation, knowledge and care.

Today, our small team of destination specialists designs journeys across six continents. We remain proudly independent, allowing us to recommend only what we genuinely love.

Our promises
