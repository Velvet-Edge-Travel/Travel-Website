01

Who we are

Velvet Edge Travel (referred to as “Velvet Edge Travel”, “we”, “us” or “our”) designs and arranges tailor-made holidays and related travel services. For the personal information described in this policy, Velvet Edge Travel is the data controller unless we explain otherwise at the time your information is collected.

This means we decide why and how your personal information is used. Travel providers that deliver part of your journey may also act as independent controllers under their own privacy notices. If your booking documents identify a separate legal contracting entity, those documents will explain its role.

Privacy contact

Email bookings@velvetedgetravel.com, write “Privacy” in the subject line, or call +44 (0)7957 644 502.

02

When this policy applies

This policy applies when you:

  • browse or interact with the Velvet Edge Travel website;
  • save a journey in your browser;
  • send a travel enquiry by website, email, telephone or social media;
  • ask us to create a proposal, quotation or itinerary;
  • make or discuss a booking, payment, amendment or cancellation;
  • travel on an arrangement we have helped to organise;
  • contact us for assistance, feedback, a complaint or an emergency;
  • subscribe to or engage with marketing, if we offer it; or
  • act for a supplier, partner, travel professional or other business contact.

It does not govern websites or services operated independently by airlines, hotels, insurers, payment providers, destination partners or other third parties. Their privacy notices apply to their own processing.

04

Where information comes from

We may receive personal information:

  • directly from you, including through an enquiry, call, email or booking;
  • from the lead traveller, organiser, family member or authorised representative;
  • from travel advisers, corporate clients or referral partners;
  • from airlines, hotels, tour operators, destination partners and other suppliers;
  • from payment, fraud-prevention or identity-verification providers;
  • from public sources where relevant to a business relationship; and
  • automatically through hosting and browser technologies when you use the website.

05

UK data protection law requires a lawful basis for each use. The bases most relevant to our work are taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, complying with law, pursuing legitimate interests and consent.

Establish, exercise or defend legal claims Legitimate interests and, where applicable, legal claims conditions under data protection law.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider the need for the processing, its benefit, its impact on you and the safeguards available. We do not use personal information for a new, incompatible purpose without a valid legal basis and appropriate notice.

06

Health, accessibility and other sensitive information

Travel can require information about mobility, health, allergies, dietary needs, religion or other sensitive circumstances. We ask for this only when relevant to your request, safety or the proper delivery of a service. We will normally rely on your explicit consent to use and share it with the providers that need it.

In a serious emergency, we may use information to protect vital interests where consent cannot reasonably be obtained. We may also process it where needed for legal claims or another specific condition allowed by law. You may withdraw consent, but doing so can limit our ability to arrange or safely provide a requested service.

At the enquiry stage

Do not place passport details, payment-card information or detailed medical information in the website enquiry form or an unprotected email. We will explain an appropriate channel if those details become necessary.

07

Children and young travellers

Our website and enquiry service are intended for adults. We may need a child's name, age, date of birth, passport information, dietary needs or assistance requirements to arrange family travel. This information should be supplied by a parent, guardian or authorised lead traveller and will be used only where needed for the journey, safety or legal requirements.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information without appropriate authority, please contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

08

Who we share information with

We share only what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. Recipients may include:

  • airlines, hotels, villas, cruise lines, rail operators and transfer providers;
  • tour operators, destination management companies, guides, golf courses and experience providers;
  • visa, passport, insurance, medical-assistance or emergency-support providers where requested or required;
  • payment processors, banks, accountants and fraud-prevention providers;
  • website hosting, email, communications, customer-management, document and IT-support providers;
  • professional advisers, insurers, auditors and legal representatives;
  • regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, border agencies or tax authorities where lawfully required;
  • a buyer, investor or successor if our business or assets are reorganised, subject to confidentiality safeguards; and
  • other parties where you ask us to share information or give valid consent.

Some recipients process information on our instructions and must protect it under contract. Travel providers often decide for themselves how to use booking information and are independent controllers. Their notices should be read alongside this one. We do not sell your personal information.

09

International travel and data transfers

Tailor-made international travel necessarily involves sending booking information to countries outside the United Kingdom. Some may not provide privacy protections equivalent to UK law. A hotel, airline, guide, border authority or destination partner may need your details to provide the journey you request.

Where UK transfer rules apply to a provider acting on our instructions, we use an available safeguard such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses, or another lawful mechanism. Where a transfer is necessary to perform your contract or take requested pre-contract steps, the law may permit that transfer. Contact us if you would like more information about the safeguard relevant to a particular transfer.

How long we keep information

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, including service, legal, accounting, tax, insurance, fraud-prevention and claims requirements. We consider the amount, sensitivity and risk of the information when setting periods.

RecordTypical approach
Unbooked enquiries and proposalsNormally retained for up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact, unless you ask us to keep planning with you or a dispute requires longer.
Booking, payment and travel-service recordsNormally retained for 7 years after the end of the relevant financial year or journey, reflecting tax, accounting and potential claims requirements.
Passport and similar travel-document copiesDeleted or securely disposed of when no longer required for the trip, supplier or legal purpose; we avoid keeping copies with the general client record unnecessarily.
Health and special-category detailsKept only for as long as needed for the journey, assistance, incident or related legal requirement, then deleted or minimised.
Marketing preferencesKept while you remain subscribed; suppression records may be retained so we continue to respect an opt-out.
Security and technical logsKept for a limited period appropriate to security, diagnostics and abuse prevention.

A different period may apply where law, an active complaint, litigation, fraud concerns or a regulator requires it. When records are no longer needed, we delete, anonymise or securely dispose of them.

11

How we protect information

We use proportionate organisational and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration and disclosure. Measures may include access controls, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit, secure service providers, staff confidentiality, backups, monitoring, supplier checks and incident procedures.

No internet or email transmission is completely secure. Please use the channel we specify for passports, payment details or sensitive travel information, keep your own devices and accounts secure, and tell us promptly if you suspect a communication or booking record has been compromised.

We do not currently make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means. If that changes, we will provide the information and safeguards required by law.

12

Marketing and your choices

We may send travel inspiration, news or offers where you have asked for them or where applicable law permits us to contact an existing client about similar services. You can opt out at any time using an unsubscribe method in the message or by contacting us. Opting out of marketing does not stop essential service or booking communications.

We do not sell personal information or share it with unrelated businesses for their own direct marketing. If we propose a joint promotion requiring information sharing, we will explain it and obtain any consent required.

13

Website technology, saved journeys and external services

Travel enquiries

The current enquiry form prepares a travel brief and, by default, opens your own email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. You can alternatively download the brief. If a secure web-form provider is connected in future, the form may send your details to that provider for delivery to us; this policy and the on-page notice will be updated to identify the actual handling.

Saved journeys and browser storage

When you save a journey, the site stores only its internal holiday identifier in your browser's local storage. This is functional storage used to remember your selection on that device. It does not store your name or enquiry details and is not sent to us. You can remove saved journeys through the site or clear local site data in your browser settings.

Hosting, images and fonts

The website may be delivered through GitHub Pages. As with most hosting services, technical request and security logs may be processed by the host. Pages currently request selected images from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser, requested resource and time of request under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not set advertising cookies and does not include a website analytics service. Essential hosting or external-resource providers may process technical request data. If we add non-essential cookies, analytics, advertising or similar tracking, we will update this notice and provide consent controls where required.

Links to other websites

External links are provided for convenience. We do not control third-party websites, applications or social networks and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their notices before providing personal information.

14

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK law may give you the right to:

  • ask whether we process your personal information and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to erase information where there is no good reason to keep it;
  • restrict how information is used in certain circumstances;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests or to direct marketing;
  • receive information you supplied in a portable, machine-readable form where applicable;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier lawful processing;
  • ask for safeguards relating to solely automated decisions, if any are introduced; and
  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

To exercise a right, email us using the privacy contact above and describe your request. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and protect your records. We normally respond within one month, although the law allows more time for a complex request. Rights are not absolute; if an exemption applies, we will explain our decision where permitted.
