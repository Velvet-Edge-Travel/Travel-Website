A little clarity

Before you go.

Good journeys begin with the right questions.

Can I change the itinerary?

Yes. These journeys are starting points. Tell us your preferred dates, pace, room arrangements and interests in your enquiry.

How do I get a quotation?

Choose a journey or tell us your ideas, preferred dates and who is travelling. We will prepare a tailored proposal with availability, accommodation, travel arrangements and your personal quotation. Every holiday is designed around you.

Does an enquiry commit me to booking?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. It is not a booking, reservation or payment.

Can golfers and non-golfers travel together?

Tell us who is travelling and what each person enjoys. Include your golf preferences and ideas for the days beyond the course.

What should I include in my enquiry?

A destination or idea, approximate dates, number of travellers and an optional budget are a useful start. You do not need passport or payment details.
