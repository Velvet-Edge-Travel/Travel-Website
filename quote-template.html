01 Day by day Itinerary overview
Day 1Monday 12 October

Arrive in the Algarve

Fly from London Gatwick to Faro. Your private driver will meet you in arrivals for the 25-minute journey to Conrad Algarve. Settle into your Deluxe King Pool View room and enjoy the remainder of the afternoon at leisure.

Day 2Tuesday 13 October

Quinta do Lago South

Breakfast at the hotel followed by a private transfer to Quinta do Lago South. Tee time at 10:10 with a shared buggy. Return transfer and afternoon at leisure.

Day 3Wednesday 14 October

A day at your own pace

A free day to enjoy the spa and pools, explore the Ria Formosa or visit the old town of Faro. Our concierge can reserve lunch or dinner on request.

Day 4Thursday 15 October

Monte Rei Golf & Country Club

Private transfer to Monte Rei for an 11:00 tee time on the Jack Nicklaus Signature course. Shared buggy and range balls included. Return to the hotel after your round.

Day 5Friday 16 October

Algarve coast

Day at leisure. An optional private boat charter through the coastal caves and hidden beaches can be arranged.

Day 6Saturday 17 October

Vale do Lobo Royal

Morning transfer for your final round, including the celebrated cliff-top 16th hole. Tee time at 09:40 with a shared buggy, followed by a relaxed return to the hotel.

Day 7Sunday 18 October

A final day in the sun

Your final full day is free for the beach, spa or a long lunch overlooking the Atlantic.

Day 8Monday 19 October

Departure

Breakfast and check-out. Private transfer to Faro Airport for your return flight to London Gatwick.
