Before a payment link goes out

A few details worth checking

  • Open and verify the HTTPS address, recipient, currency and exact instalment amount.
  • Use Revolut Business for payment collection. This tracker never asks for or stores card details, and does not automatically sync payment status.
  • Share the confirmed booking, cancellation and refund terms from the traveller’s quote.
  • Tell the traveller what payment confirmation or receipt they should expect.
  • Import the original quote HTML or JSON draft so every traveller is attached to its quote reference.
  • Create an encrypted link for one traveller. Send its passphrase separately; the link is a snapshot and cannot be revoked or updated.
  • Internal notes and other travellers are excluded from customer links and summaries.

For live, revocable customer access or shared team records, this static GitHub Pages setup needs an authenticated backend. Do not add traveller records or generated share links to the Git repository.
